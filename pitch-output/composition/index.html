How it works

Buy, own, sell at a fixed price.

1

The contract buys the goods

from the supplier, with the investors' money, and owns them.

→
2

It sells them to the importer

at a price fixed on day one. She pays after the sale.

→
3

Investors share the fixed profit

the difference between the two prices, split by their shares.

No interest. Nothing grows with time.

Murabaha, the oldest form of Islamic trade finance, on a public blockchain where every step can be checked.

The business model

Qafila earns only when the deal is repaid.

On this deal: 8% markup
6% to investors
2% Qafila
The fee comes out of the markup and is paid only on repayment. If the importer does not pay, Qafila earns nothing.
Qafila co-invests 5% of every deal
