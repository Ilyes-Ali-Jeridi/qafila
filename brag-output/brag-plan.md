# Brag Plan: Qafila

## What is this app?
Qafila lets anyone fund a real shipment for an African importer that banks turn down: the contract buys the goods, owns them, resells them at a fixed price, AI checks the documents against the company register, sanctions lists and live carrier data, and late fees go to charity. Built on Stellar at HackMeridian 2026.

## The angle
A caravan, on-chain. The video is a dark trading-floor ledger seen through aurora light: one hard fact about who banks say no to, then the product doing its job in four beats (a real deal card, the AI checks landing one by one, the rule that late fees go to charity), ending on the name and the domain. Nothing generic: every number, label and check is pulled from the app's own screens and copy.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant display type on obsidian with the aurora rising behind it: "Banks say no to 47% of Africa's small importers." Held long enough to read, then the aurora swells and the name lands.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Qafila wordmark at full scale with the one-line promise from the landing page: "Fund a real shipment. Own the goods. Share a fixed profit."
- A real deal card, recreated: "2,000 phone cases and screen protectors, Shenzhen (Yantian) → Lagos (Apapa)", cost 20,000 test dollars, return 6%, 90 days; the funding bar fills while the number counts up.
- The AI report: four checks arrive one by one with teal ticks ("Container check digit valid", "Company register: active", "Sanctions: no match", "Carrier: loaded at Yantian"), then the saffron "Approved, risk 100" badge.
- The rule: "Late fees go to charity. Never to investors." over a receipt line from the charity ledger: "Late fee · 50.00 test dollars · tx 6c10…" with a stellar.expert link look.

## Outro / punchline
"Qafila" at scale, "qafila.dev", and a small line "Built on Stellar at HackMeridian 2026". The aurora settles, the bell rings once.

## User flow worth showing
Importer uploads documents → the AI report approves with evidence per check → investors fund the deal with a passkey → the importer repays at the fixed price and a late fee reaches charity. Scenes 3 and 4 are the centrepiece: the report checks landing and the charity receipt.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a caravan on-chain; dark fintech ledger with aurora light, confident and quiet
- Interpretation: four scenes, slow crossfades, big type with generous holds, motion from light and counters rather than from cuts; no jokes, no hype words, every line is a fact from the product.
