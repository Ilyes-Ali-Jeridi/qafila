How it works · murabaha, the oldest form of Islamic trade finance

Buy the goods. Own them. Sell at a fixed price.

01 VerifyThree documents, checked by AI

Invoice, packing list, supplier licence, against each other, the company register, sanctions lists and live ship tracking. A person signs off.

02 FundImporter first, then investors

She locks a 20% deposit. Investors fund the shipment and get locked share tokens.

03 ShipThe contract pays the supplier

The bill of lading is made out to Qafila. An agent confirms the loading event and pays the balance.

04 RepayFixed price, fixed profit

She buys the goods at the price fixed on day one. Investors claim principal plus profit. Late fees go to charity.

The rules the contract enforces

No interest. Real goods, real trade.

  • The deal owns real goods before it sells them.
  • Profit comes from a sale at a price fixed at the start. Nothing grows with time.
  • The importer puts money in first and loses it first if investors are let down.
  • Late fees go to charity, never to investors or to Qafila. Public ledger at qafila.dev/charity.
  • Shares cannot be traded before the deal ends: the issuer is locked, only the contract can move them.

Why Stellar · what we integrated

Built on Stellar, end to end.

Soroban contract (Rust)

Promise, list, invest, pay supplier, confirm shipment, sale, repay, late fee, claim, default, charity sweep. 49 tests, property tests over the Sharia rules.

One share asset per deal

Classic asset with AUTH_REQUIRED, AUTH_REVOCABLE and CLAWBACK; the issuer's admin is the contract and its master key weight is 0.

Passkey smart accounts

OpenZeppelin accounts, Qafila's relayer pays the fees. A restricted smart account runs the shipment agent.

Paid API over x402

Other lenders' agents buy a signed verification for 0.50 USDC; the attestation verifies on-chain.

AI that never decides alone

Qwen on AWS Bedrock reads the documents; deterministic checks, GLEIF, OpenSanctions and carrier tracking decide with a written policy.

Everything linked

Every on-chain action in the app links to stellar.expert. Every AI report is signed by a published verifier key.
